HOW IT WORKS

Unlike size-based alerts (which fire on any 100K+ trade), the anomaly engine compares each trade to the rolling session baseline.

A 5x trade when average size is 2,000 XRP matters more than a 100K trade when average is 80,000 XRP.

It also detects velocity bursts — when multiple anomalous trades cluster together in under 90 seconds. This often precedes price movement.

The Drift Compass updates when 3+ timeframes align in the same direction. That's the rare moment when momentum is genuinely stacking up.
